Privacy

28to3.me operates this public website and sells Actionscope — GitHub Actions minute visibility — plus Afterglow, SophPDF Debloat, and Soph Explorer Debloat. This page is a short policy for the 28to3.me Stripe business profile.

What we collect

This static site does not set cookies or run an analytics pixel. If you email us or pay the $49 booking fee for a Minute Teardown, we receive the information you send (name, email, organization, payment details processed by Stripe).

The free Action

Actionscope runs in your GitHub Actions workflow and reads jobs for the current run with your GITHUB_TOKEN. It writes a Job Summary in that run. 28to3.me does not receive your repository contents or workflow logs from the free Action.

Soph Explorer Debloat

Soph Explorer Debloat is free forever. There is no Stripe checkout. Download is from Sofa-Loaf/soph-explorer.

Afterglow

Afterglow is free. Android and Play first; iOS later. There is no Stripe checkout and no account. The Play listing policy is apps/afterglow.html#privacy.

v0 collects precise location, microphone audio, and photos only for the feature — leave-detect, the ~50ft / 15m listen gate, an 8–12s voice note, or a still. Data is stored on the device, is not sold, and is not shared. Background location is off. Source: Sofa-Loaf/afterglow.

Payments

Card payments for the $49 Minute Teardown booking fee and the $0.99 Minute Cheat Sheet are handled by Stripe through the Payment Links on this site. SophPDF Debloat is $29 one-time when a Stripe Payment Link exists; that checkout is not live yet. Soph Explorer Debloat and Afterglow have no payment.
